- **Severity**: `P1`
- **Category**: `WRONG ART`

- **Build Identity**: `ra-c9c273ff91a4-20261005062000` (RC3-play)
- **Reproduction Steps**:
  1. Launch game in Chromium mobile viewport (390×844).
  2. Execute `RAAdventureScene.begin('NEW_OGA_M7')` or trigger mission 7 arrival.
  3. Observe Gbenga actor staged in slot `right`.
- **Screenshot Evidence**: 
  - Actual scene: `docs/rc4/visual/evidence/bosses_factions/39_gbenga_m7_dinner_defect.png`
  - Actual crop: `docs/rc4/visual/evidence/side_by_side/gbenga_actual_rendered.png`
  - Expected approved asset: `docs/rc4/visual/evidence/side_by_side/gbenga_expected_approved.png`
- **Expected Approved Asset**:
  - `assets/before_the_fame/characters/gbenga/runtime_80x96/gbenga_neutral_anchor_80x96_v2.png` (SHA-256: `3b89b9e67175949fc1e2ee04c26a0c5c32c451528654ff9cb0db32e609341492`)
  - Authority: BUILD-4 / OL-039 taste pass recorded in `art_department/APPROVAL_LEDGER.md` line 241 ("corrected Gbenga v2 over v1").
- **Actual Loaded Asset**:
  - Procedural `<canvas>` generated by `RAPixel.drawActor(c, person.look, 40, 88, 1)` with skin `#6b4423` and top `#ffffff`. Tagged `.adv-placeholder-actor`.
- **Concrete Observation**:
  Gbenga is the primary crime boss and head of NEW OGA. In every scene he appears, a generic, flat brown-and-white silhouette is drawn on an 80×96 canvas. However, the fully polished, chunky pixel Gbenga v2 sprite (complete with sunglasses, patterned agbada, and necklace) is present in the packaged build.
- **Player Impact**: Severe loss of narrative climax and visual polish during major storyline boss confrontations.
- **Smallest Proposed Repair**: Add `gbenga` entry to `window.RAArtRegistry.characters` pointing `anchor` to `assets/before_the_fame/characters/gbenga/runtime_80x96/gbenga_neutral_anchor_80x96_v2.png` and registering states (`adjusting_sleeves`, `defeated`, `golden_draco`, `my_son`, `voice_note`).
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED`
- **Related Finding**: STOVE B coverage defect for `NEW_OGA_M7`.
