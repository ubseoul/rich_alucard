# RC5 polish voice rules (OL-083/084)

Applied on `rc5/overlord-polish` (public + HQ). Mirror these on sealed lines.

1. **Untouchable:** Ube's RC3 sheet lines (`tools/rc3/voice-sheet.json`), `docs/rc2/PROTECTED_LINES.md`, Rich's recorded-style lines (capitalized "brother." / "shi." / "best we can shit." sets) and frozen art. Move them, frame them, never reword them.
2. **Max 3 sentences per box.** Split, don't pad. Fragments count as sentences.
3. **Narration and guidance are lowercase, blunt, present tense.** "ogun throwing a rave. pull up before you clock in." Not "Your invite is ready."
4. **Kill system voice.** No "RESOLVING", "NOT AVAILABLE YET", "ROUND COMPLETE", "Advance one day". Say what it means in Rich's mouth: "RUN? THIS YOUR HOUSE."
5. **Keep numbers and rules legible.** Jokes ride next to the number, never replace it: "-16 HP. THAT BRIEFCASE GOT EQUITY".
6. **One joke per box.** If the second clause explains the first, cut it.
7. **Don't glue quotes to tutorials.** An Ube line gets its own line. The instruction goes on the next one.
8. **Slang is real, not cosplay.** "dont", "gotta", "bro", "oga", "damn"; no apostrophe polish on Rich. Other characters keep their own register (Adeoluwa formal, aunties loud, Granny "child").
9. **Club stays sexy, never explicit.** Dancers are 21+ and say so on screen. Money is the flirt.
10. **A payoff names the number.** Every spend ends on what it bought: PAID amount, hits, and her reaction.
11. **Labels are verbs.** MAKE IT RAIN, RUN IT BACK, LEAVE. Max ~3 words.
12. **Encoding:** UTF-8 only. A lone Latin-1 byte (e.g. `·`) renders as � on phones.
