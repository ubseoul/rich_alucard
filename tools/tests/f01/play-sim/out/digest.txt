Definitions. Story beat = a memorable-event family with weight ≥ 6 that shows up in ≤ 25% of all PLAYs (routine stingers cannot pass as variety). Visible cause: broad = any cause the player can see on screen (trait, choice, greed, car, weapon, seat, relationship, an earlier visible event, a card hazard word, an enemy tell); luck-only swings are counted as unexplained.
