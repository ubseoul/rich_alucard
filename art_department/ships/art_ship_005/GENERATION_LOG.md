# Generation and Promotion Log — Art Ship 005

## Candidate production

The accepted set was produced in seven validated candidate packages: Waves AB, C, D, E, F, G and H. The complete 52-candidate UBE / HQ Taste & Identity Review Board exposed every candidate at the required native scale; characters also included temporary approximately 1.85x nearest-neighbor gameplay previews, and small props/creature included integer nearest-neighbor readability previews.

## Promotion

After UBE TASTE PASS for the complete batch and explicit HQ promotion authorization, the 52 accepted native PNGs were copied byte-for-byte to canonical paths under `assets/before_the_fame/`. The promotion made no ImageGen calls and performed no decoding, re-encoding, resizing, retouching, palette conversion, optimization, reinterpretation or alternate-state creation.

Raw generations, exploratory material and candidate-folder duplicates are excluded from the committed approved corpus. The canonical files named by `ART_SHIP_MANIFEST.json` are the frozen authority.

## Boundaries

The engineering demand map was inspected read-only. No SEALED/HQ-only content was accessed. No runtime code or game data was changed.
