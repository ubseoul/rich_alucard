## Quality boundary

The safe throughput boundary in this session was derivative state work from high-contrast frozen masters plus compact UI/vehicle silhouettes. Native reduction remained coherent at 80×96, 16×28, and 24×24. I stopped before bulk new-character identity invention and before more architectural environments: those would cross from controlled derivation into identity/environment design and increase regeneration risk.

Environment variants are full-frame candidates derived from frozen plates and then constrained to the exact master palette. Geometry is recognizable but should receive HQ side-by-side art review before promotion because generative edits are not pixel-identical overlays.
