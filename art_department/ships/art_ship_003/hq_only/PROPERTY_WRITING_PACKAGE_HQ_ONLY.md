## Shannon voice card

**Core read:** competent, dry, adult, practical. She sells facts, documents absurdity, and turns defects into leverage.  
**Avoid:** breathless exposition, generic sarcasm, flirting as default, instant devotion to Rich, meme slang, constant punchlines, invented family/career lore.

## Dialogue presentation notes

- Spoken dialogue must show `speaker` separately from text. Do not embed `SHANNON:` or `RICH:` inside narration.
- Narration has no speaker portrait and must not imply speech.
- Use one bottom/edge-anchored readable treatment consistent with the shared game grammar, or a short speaker-attached bubble if Engineering's dialogue system establishes it.
- Never place continuous narration over Shannon's face, Rich's face, the maintenance opening, or the active rat.
- One short line per advance is preferred during rat staging so the visible state can change with the words.
- Choices are actions, not automatically spoken Rich dialogue.
- No line in this file grants canon approval to Rich voice-pass candidates.
