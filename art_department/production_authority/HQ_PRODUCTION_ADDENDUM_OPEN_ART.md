# HQ Production Addendum v1 — Art-facing OPEN execution subset

This is an Art-facing subset of `Rich_Alucard_BTF_HQ_Production_Addendum_v1`. It does not replace the full source; it records only the execution rules future Art Agents need. No SEALED or HQ-only payload is reproduced.

## Preservation and authority

- Preserve the Claude volumes and current canon. The addendum overrides only explicit production and repository-fact items it names.
- Authority order for Art: Ube canon → `docs/CURRENT_CANON.md` → approved/frozen pixels → applicable OPEN source subset → Art Department judgment.
- Frozen pixels are immutable. New work is a bounded Art Ship; it must derive from the frozen master, not from a previous derivative.
- Art cannot approve itself, redefine a stage contract, or turn exploratory/candidate pixels into style authority.
- Runtime acceptance is separate from Art acceptance/freeze.

## Display and composition

- Primary gameplay characters target approximately **1.85×** historical on-screen presentation with nearest-neighbor filtering.
- This is an Engineering runtime rule. Never resize or redraw native source sprites to satisfy it.
- Compose new 270×480 environments with a generous readable floor band for two or three actors plus the dialogue box. Contacts, anchors, layer origins, and collision remain Engineering concerns.
- Playtesting may revise the multiplier; a future agent must not bake a new multiplier into source art.

## Production gates

- One coherent Art Ship at a time. Record scope, inputs, permission boundary, spoiler class, deliverables, stop point, and exact paths/hashes.
- Submit native files plus deterministic review boards, state definitions, Engineering mappings, and validation.
- HQ/Ube acceptance is required before `APPROVED MASTER`; freeze is an explicit subsequent decision.
- A delta to frozen art requires allowed-region scope, source hashes, unchanged-region evidence, and fresh review.
- Keep candidate/review/generation records for provenance, but never use them as default style references.

## Restricted boundary

Do not inspect, quote, summarize, import, or visually solve Vol 4, Vol 5-S, HQ-only deltas, HQ-M01/M02/M03, or any sealed reveal/identity. Use neutral repository-visible slots only. If a requested state would expose restricted canon, stop and report it as `SEALED / DO NOT TOUCH`.
