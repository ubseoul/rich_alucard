## Standing Rules

1. **OPEN Visual Concept Cards are identity authority.**
2. **Discretion within boundaries:** When a card is silent on exact face, skin tone, hair, or minor palette details, normal art-director discretion may fill the gap inside MUST / MUST NOT boundaries.
3. **Hierarchy of Truth:** Frozen pixels outrank prose for established visual grammar. Asset existence is not approval.
4. **Adult Age Rule:** All human characters are adults (21+). No child or juvenile proportions or presentation.
5. **Ube Judgment Gates:** Mama Gbenga and Auntie Grit require explicit Ube judgment before freeze.
6. **Sealed Boundary:** Do not expose, inspect, or import SEALED or HQ-only material into OPEN Art Department records.

## Production Workflow Protocol

- **Whole-Package Generation:** Default behavior for a character is:
  `SOURCE REVIEW → INTERNAL IDENTITY ANCHOR → ALL AUTHORIZED STATES → INTERNAL QA → PACKAGE → UNDERLORD / UBE GATE`. Do not stop after every single ordinary state.
- **Identity-Preserving Derivation:** Derive ordinary states from the established identity anchor. Preserve face, skin, hair, proportions, silhouette, palette identity, wardrobe identity, accessories, native pixel grammar, and contact/origin conventions.
- **Local Repair Rule:** If one state fails QA, repair only that state. Do NOT regenerate already-good states or the entire family unless the anchor itself is defective.
- **Batching:** Multiple related characters may be produced in one coherent batch when sources are complete and shared art grammar is stable. Characters must remain individually distinguishable with distinct silhouettes/body types.
- **Drift Control:** Fresh art sessions are preferred between major coherent batches rather than maintaining one indefinitely growing chat.
