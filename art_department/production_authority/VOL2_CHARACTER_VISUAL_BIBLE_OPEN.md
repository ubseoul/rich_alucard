Source: `Rich_Alucard_BTF_Vol2_Character_Visual_Bible.docx` (Vol 2, revision 1, 2026-09-24). This committed subset is the durable Art reference. PIXELS OUTRANK PROSE for measuring approved identity; frozen masters are never redesigned.

## Working rules

- All romance/sexualized characters are adults. Keep them clothed and non-graphic.
- States are minimum useful production states, not a request to create every possible animation frame.
- Incidental dressing and ordinary crowd extras remain Art Department freedom when they do not change canon.
- Spoiler classes are OPEN, GUIDED, and PLAYER-BLIND. GUIDED visuals require the named authorization; PLAYER-BLIND material is not for this area.
- Primary character presentation is approximately 185% nearest-neighbor at runtime. Preserve native cells, proportions, contacts, palette logic, and source density.
- Shared grammar: one strong silhouette idea, two or three material tones, sparse identity pixels, low fold density, compact hands and shoes, no illustrated facial rendering. Rich's large dark hair/shades, green earring and fang marks remain the primary identity anchor.
